Brand overview

Zirven is a technology and software company. We create, own and keep improving digital products, and we build custom software for partners.

For businesses ready to move forward, Zirven builds intelligent digital products: SaaS platforms, AI-powered business software, and web and mobile applications. They are engineered as products, owned for the long term and improved with every release.

What we build

  • SaaS products: multi-tenant platforms we own and operate
  • AI-powered business software: assistants, extraction, decision support
  • Web and mobile applications
  • Automation, APIs and integrations
  • Data, analytics and cloud platforms
  • Enterprise and custom software (Zirven Solutions)

Who it is for

  • Business leaders who need software that runs operations, not slides about it
  • Teams who use our products every day
  • Developers and partners who integrate with our APIs
  • Talent who want to build serious products
  • Investors who back a product company with a platform

The name

Zirve is Turkish for summit. For Zirven the summit is not a finish line. It stands for progress, for solving difficult problems, for reaching the next level and for products that keep evolving. Every release starts from the height the last one reached.

About V2.1

V2.1 defines Zirven as a technology and software company and the parent brand of a product portfolio. It keeps V2's visual craft: the Summit Z, the purple palette, Readex Pro, the contour pattern and the token and SVG production system. It retires V2's consumer positioning, its taglines, its warm accent colour and its consumer interface concepts. That earlier concept could return one day as a separate product under Zirven, but it is not the corporate identity.

03

Creative direction

The big idea: the next level. A summit is a point you reach and then build from. Every Zirven product release climbs from the last.

1 · One angle, used with precision

The 20° ramp of the Summit Z is the only geometric motif: in the overline marker, the ascent lines, the summit cut and the way product windows step up a layout.

2 · The product is the hero

Show real interfaces, real data and real workflows, built with the design system. They prove that we build software. Mock-ups beat metaphors.

3 · A dark stage with violet light

Zirven Night is the stage. Violet and Electric Purple are the light on it. Light surfaces (Snow) are used for documents, print and long reading.

4 · Restraint, with one signal

Intelligence Cyan appears only where intelligence or live data is at work: an AI answer, a live sync, an active node. It makes up less than 5% of any layout.

5 · Systems, not ornaments

The supporting graphics show how software works: contour data terrain, module grids, layered platforms and connected nodes.

6 · Calm density

Interfaces are information-rich but quiet. Use hairlines instead of heavy boxes, tabular numbers, generous gutters and an 8 px rhythm.

Endorsement rules

  • Product lockup: product tile + name | "a product by" + Zirven logo.
  • Product websites end with "VEVO is a product by Zirven".
  • App stores and contracts list Zirven Technology as publisher.
  • The Zirven logo never contains a product glyph, and a product never uses the Summit Z.

Naming products

  • Short (4–6 letters), easy to say in EN, TR and AR.
  • No "Zirven" or "Z-" prefix, so the portfolio can grow freely.
  • Features take descriptive names: [Product] + plain noun.
  • Clear trademarks in classes 9, 35 and 42 before launch.

Product family system

  • Every product icon uses the Zirven tile: a rounded square (22.5% radius) on Night or Deep Violet.
  • Products are told apart by glyph and name, not by new colours.
  • Every product uses the Zirven UI system, tokens and Readex Pro.
  • Cyan stays reserved for AI and live data in every product.
05

The Summit Z

A capital Z drawn as a climb, a summit and a solid base. One shape that is a letter, a direction and a promise to keep moving up.

Reading the mark. The ramp climbs at 20°, the steady progress of a product getting better. It reaches an acute summit, the next level. The descent drops at 55° to a solid base, the platform everything is built on. The wordmark is set in lowercase Readex Pro SemiBold, outlined, so the capital Z of the symbol is never repeated.

07

Lockups & languages

One international master brand, fully usable in Turkish and Arabic. Use English by default. Use Turkish and Arabic lockups where the audience reads those languages.

Writing the name

  • Running text: Zirven (capital Z).
  • Legal: Zirven Technology / Zirven Teknoloji.
  • Never ZIRVEN in Turkish capitals. If capitals are unavoidable, write ZİRVEN with the dotted İ.

Why lowercase

Turkish has two letters i: dotted (i/İ) and dotless (ı/I). In capitals, ZIRVEN reads as "Zırven" in Turkish. The lowercase wordmark reads correctly everywhere, in every market.

Arabic

زيرفن is set in Readex Pro Arabic, weight 560, and outlined. In RTL layouts the symbol sits on the right and the 20° ramp is not mirrored: the symbol is a letterform, not an arrow.

14

Pattern & graphic devices

V2's summit contour pattern stays, re-read as data terrain: elevation lines that are also a field of data. Three supporting devices complete the system.

Ascent lines

Parallel lines at 20°. Use them for glass manifestation, section backgrounds and signage bands.

Summit marker

A short rule climbing at 20° before every overline. It is the smallest brand signature.

What we build

Summit cut

A 20° chamfer on one top-right corner of a hero card or a slide. Use it at most once per layout.

Patterns stay quiet: line opacity 10–35%, never behind body text, and never more than one pattern per layout.

16

Imagery

Our imagery proves that we build software. The order of preference: the product itself, abstract system graphics, then photography of real people doing real work.

1 · Product: real interfaces from our design system
2 · System graphics: data terrain, modules, layers
3 · Photography: teams at work, natural light, cool violet grade

Photography direction

  • Real teams in real workplaces: engineering reviews, whiteboards, operations floors, warehouses, clinics, offices.
  • Natural light, honest moments, people focused on work, never looking at the camera.
  • Architecture and engineering detail: precise lines, concrete, glass, steel.
  • Grade: cool shadows lifted toward Deep Violet, true skin tones, low saturation.
  • Diverse, international casting.

Never

  • Robots, humanoid AI, brains, glowing orbs, "digital faces".
  • Circuit boards, binary rain, holograms, crypto coins.
  • Stock handshakes, pointing at screens, staged high fives.
  • Food, retail or leisure scenes as brand imagery.
  • Mountain-climbing photography used to illustrate "summit". The metaphor lives in the geometry.

Show AI through what it produces: an answer with sources, an automated step, a decision made faster. Never draw AI as a creature or a sphere.

UI design language

One interface system for every Zirven product. It is calm, dense where data needs it, dark-first, and readable in English, Turkish and Arabic.

Data first

Numbers and states lead. Chrome recedes into hairlines and surface steps.

One signal

Cyan marks AI and live data, nothing else. Users learn it once.

People in control

AI suggests, people approve. Actions that change data are always reviewable.

8 px rhythm

A 4 px sub-grid, a 248 px sidebar, 12-column web layouts with 24 px gutters.

AI patterns

  • Cyan spark label on every AI-generated block.
  • Sources are always cited (queries, files, records).
  • "Requires approval" on actions that change data.
  • Show progress steps, not spinners.

Data visualisation

  • Series order: Electric, Lavender, ink-500, Deep Violet.
  • Cyan only for AI forecasts or live series.
  • Gridlines at 8% Lavender. Tabular numbers.
  • Sequential scale: Deep Violet → Electric → Lavender.

Themes & motion

  • Dark is the default for products. Light is available for documents and reports.
  • Ease cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1): 120 / 200 / 360 ms.
  • Reveals climb 10 px along the ramp. Respect reduced motion.

Voice & messaging

Write the way a senior engineer explains a product to a smart customer: specific, calm and sure.

Write

  • "VEVO answers questions about your operations, with sources."
  • "Actions that change data wait for approval."
  • "We build in short, measured steps."
  • "API-first. SSO, roles and audit logs included."

Don't write

  • "Revolutionizing business with next-generation AI."
  • "Unleash the power of seamless synergy."
  • "The future of everything, today."
  • "World-class, cutting-edge, game-changing solutions."

Logo review (V2.1)

We tested the Summit Z again for a software company, against a modular and a layered alternative, at 16 to 64 px, as an app icon, in a website header and in monochrome.

A is the strongest at 16 px, in monochrome and in the portfolio, and it is the easiest to remember. B's modules became the logo's motion. C's layers became the platform layer in architecture diagrams. Full comparison: logo/exploration/logo-review.html.
