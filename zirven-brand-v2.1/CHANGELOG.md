# Changelog

## V2.1 — October 2026 · Business positioning corrected

V2 presented Zirven as a consumer-facing local marketplace. That was the wrong business. V2.1 defines Zirven as a **technology and software company** and the **parent brand** of a product portfolio. It keeps V2's visual craft and replaces its strategy, copy, icons and applications.

### Retained from V2 (kept or refined, not redesigned)
- **Summit Z** symbol and its geometric direction, including the **20° summit ramp**. Refined: the angles now relate (20° ramp + 35° summit = 55° descent), stroke weights are balanced, and a pixel-snapped **micro master** is used at 24 px and below.
- **Palette foundation:** Zirven Night, Zirven Violet, Electric Purple, Lavender, plus Deep Violet and Snow, with dark and light systems.
- **Readex Pro**, still the best single family for Latin + Turkish + Arabic. JetBrains Mono added for code and data.
- **Light and dark logo system**, favicon and app-icon system, clear-space and minimum-size rules.
- **Summit / contour pattern**, re-read as "data terrain".
- **Production system:** generated SVGs, CSS/JSON design tokens and generation scripts.

### Changed
- **Positioning:** Zirven builds intelligent digital products (Software · SaaS · AI) for an international B2B audience. It is the parent brand, VEVO is its first product, and Zirven Solutions builds custom software.
- **Primary line:** "Building intelligent digital products." (TR: "Akıllı dijital ürünler geliştiriyoruz." · AR: "نبني منتجات رقمية ذكية").
- **Brand architecture:** endorsed model. Product lockup "VEVO — a product by Zirven", a shared product-tile family and slots for future products.
- **Summit meaning:** progress, solving hard problems, the next level and product evolution.
- **Wordmark:** lowercase `zirven`. Capital ZIRVEN reads as "Zırven" to Turkish readers (dotless I). Lowercase reads correctly in every language and does not repeat the symbol's capital Z.
- **Accent:** the warm V2 accent (Summit Coral) is retired. A restricted cool accent, **Intelligence Cyan**, is used only for AI activity and live data.
- **Icon system:** the V2 consumer icon set is replaced by 16 technology icons (Products, SaaS, AI, Automation, API, Integrations, Cloud, Analytics, Data, Database, Web, Mobile, Security, Teams, Workflow, AI Assistant) and 36 UI icons.
- **Guidelines rewritten:** 01 Brand overview, 02 Brand personality, 03 Creative direction, 16 Imagery, 17 UI design language, 18 Social media, 19 Print, 20 Digital, 21 Design tokens. Added 04 Brand architecture, 22 Voice & messaging, 23 Logo review and 24 Files & production.

### Removed
- V2's taglines in all three languages, its consumer-marketplace vocabulary, venue categories, savings messaging, consumer UI cards and badges, regional-audience targeting and competitor comparison.

### Added
- **Logo review:** three candidates (A refined, B modular, C layered) compared at 16, 24, 32 and 64 px, as app icons, in a website header and in monochrome. **A was selected.** B's modules became the logo motion and C's layers became the platform layer in architecture diagrams.
- **Twenty applications:** corporate website, homepage hero, products page, product ecosystem, VEVO product page, SaaS dashboard, AI assistant, analytics, automation, mobile, API & integrations, business card, letterhead, email signature, LinkedIn post, social announcement set, office signage, pitch deck cover, GitHub / developer branding, app icon & favicon.
- **New brand board** built to read as technology, software, SaaS, AI and digital products.
- **Semantic audit:** `scripts/audit.py` and `audit/semantic-audit.md`.
