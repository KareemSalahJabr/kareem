FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else on your mind? Ask during the demo or write to vevo@zirven.com.

How long does it take to go live?

Most teams are live in four to six weeks. Connecting core systems takes one to two weeks; the rest is setting up workspaces, roles and the first workflows with our onboarding team.

Where is our data hosted?

In the region you choose when the workspace is created: the EU (Frankfurt), Türkiye (Istanbul) or the Middle East (UAE). Data and backups stay in that region.

Does VEVO use our data to train AI models?

No. Your data is never used to train models, ours or any provider’s. The assistant only reads what the person asking can already see, and every answer lists its sources.

Can VEVO connect to our ERP?

Yes. Prebuilt connectors cover SAP S/4HANA, Microsoft Dynamics 365, Oracle NetSuite and others. For in-house systems, use the REST API and webhooks, or Zirven Solutions can build the connector for you.
