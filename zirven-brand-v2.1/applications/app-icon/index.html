The app icon master is a 1024 px tile with a Deep Violet → Zirven Violet gradient running along the 20° ramp and the Snow Summit Z at 56% of the tile height. From 48 px down, a flat Violet tile and the micro master (base bar snapped to whole pixels) replace it. All files are generated by scripts/build_brand.py and rasterised by scripts/render.mjs.
